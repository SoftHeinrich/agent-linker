**One survivor at STRONG: Probe D — runtime coref rubric replacing static `COREF_RULES`.**

The Probe D result also EQUALS the Claude-Sonnet mediastore baseline (0.9836), closing the cross-model gap on this dataset entirely. This is the strongest single-probe signal in the wave.

## Per-Probe Sentence

- **Probe A (Voyager v4)**: R5 abstraction validator rejected 100% of R3 distillator's proposed patterns; the R3 prompt instructed textbook SE vocabulary that R5's 5-style transferability test marked as style-dependent. The two prompts are mutually inconsistent on the v4 proposal's design. Hits the proposal's explicit falsification criterion ("R5 rejects 80%+ → R3 can't produce abstract enough patterns").

- **Probe B (preamble + cached rubric)**: F1 -5.24pp; the LLM-built per-dataset alias-judge rubric was substantively reasonable and GATE-06 clean but contained an over-aggressive token-reject rule (rule 2 listed "storage, access, management, adapter, facade, cache, database, file, media, load balancer, packaging" as types to reject); produced 4 FNs vs the anchor's 1.

- **Probe C (Self-Refine)**: F1 matched anchor exactly (+0.00004pp); 80% iter-0 contested rate on mediastore (4 of 5 mappings); iter-1 fired, doubled judge cost, did not change the final approved set. WEAK_PASS by threshold, but no signal of actual benefit on this dataset.

- **Probe D (upstream coref rubric)**: F1 +1.59pp; perfect precision (P=1.0, 0 FPs); the runtime rubric suppressed the 1 coref FP that B/C still emitted under the static `COREF_RULES`. The mediastore gpt-5.4 score now equals the mediastore Claude baseline.
