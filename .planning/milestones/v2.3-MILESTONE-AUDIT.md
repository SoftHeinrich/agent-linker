## Key Findings

**1. Split-fragility: BBB as training data causes all-rollback.**
Split2 (MS+TS+BBB train): 14 proposals across 5 passes, 14 rollbacks, 0 patterns committed → empty bank. Probation gate deltas: −0.52, −0.94, −1.26, −1.51, −0.64pp. LLM variance (±3.4–3.8pp per dataset) completely swamps the signal. BBB's high FN count and sparse gold density (0.29 gold/sent for TM; 0.71 for BBB with role-referential style) make it an unreliable training signal for the current sentence-scoped axiom vocabulary.

**2. Minimal cross-split consensus: only 2 patterns generalize.**
Raw patterns: 10. After Jaccard ≥ 0.6 dedup: 8 clusters. After ≥2-split survival filter: 2 patterns in 2 slots (DOC_KNOWLEDGE_EXTRACTION_RULES + COREF_RULES). Split3 produced 8 patterns but most were too split-specific to survive the cross-split filter. The 2 surviving patterns deliver +1.6pp cross-split lift over axiom-only floor.

**3. No convergence in any split.**
All 3 splits hit the 5-pass cap without convergence (macro ≥ 0.90 on training projects). Training macros: split1 0.8941, split2 0.8855, split3 0.9561. The combination of probation gate noise and axiom vocabulary gaps (sentence-local slots cannot express document-structure failures) prevents stable learning.

**4. Probation gate is broken.**
Six compounding bugs render the gate unreliable at sub-1pp signal levels. The gate compares two independently-stochastic L-role runs; LLM variance exceeds the pattern effect in all but the easiest datasets. This is the root cause of split2's empty bank and the primary blocker for future re-runs. See debt item D-1.

**5. Axiom vocabulary ceiling reached for BBB/TM.**
14 FNs from section-context naming (SCN) and 7 FPs from responsibility-list gerunds are not addressable by the current 9 sentence-local slots. Both failure modes require document-structure or paragraph-scope awareness. See debt items D-2 and D-3.
