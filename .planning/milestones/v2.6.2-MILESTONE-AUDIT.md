## Findings (the milestone's research output)

### Finding 1 — 17a rename is safe (GPT variance confirms zero logic change)
s_linker17a (zero logic change) shows macro −2.8pp vs s15. TM gap = −0.2pp (3 links) confirms
the structural invariance. BBB and JAB gaps are within known GPT ±5-12 link run-to-run variance.
**The ICSE naming (Framing A/B/C, Knowledge Acquisition / Link Recovery / Multi-Framing Merge)
is valid and safe to use as the paper's architecture description.**

### Finding 2 — k=2 voting too conservative; union better but s15 integrated approach wins
s_linker17b (unified, k=2) shows TM −4.2pp and BBB −7.4pp regression beyond GPT variance bounds.
Root cause: BBB FN rises 17 → 19 → 22; TM FN rises 5 → 6 → 10. k=2 drops TPs appearing in only
1 framing.

s_linker17c (union merge) recovers: TM −2.5pp, BBB −0.6pp, macro −2.0pp vs s15. Union > k=2 by
+2.1pp. But union over-fires: FP rises from 31 (s15) → 43 (17c, +12). JAB −7.3pp is consistent
across ALL 17x variants — architecture-level regression from separating validation into its own
phase (coref then fires on the enlarged pool). Conclusion: the "integrated" approach of s15
(seed + entity extraction with shared axiom context) outperforms "unified" multi-framing at both
merge strategies tested.

### Finding 3 — Entity pipeline essential (confirmed again by 15b/15c)
s_linker15b (alias-recovery only): GPT 86.6% (−2.5pp), Claude 86.4% (−6.3pp).
Entity pipeline is not replaceable by alias recovery alone.

### ICSE Architecture Decision (FINAL — confirmed by 17c)
**Use s_linker17a (= s15 with 17a naming) as the paper architecture.** The renaming successfully
maps the implementation to the Framing A/B/C conceptual model. Sequential execution of alias
discovery + seed pass is an implementation optimization the paper can acknowledge, not a
conceptual limitation.

s_linker17c (union merge) confirmed this decision: even the most permissive merge strategy for the
unified design still regresses −2.0pp vs s15, with +12 FPs. The paper can present the exploration
as evidence that the integrated design is non-trivially better than either architectural variant
(17b k=2: −4.1pp; 17c union: −2.0pp). Both unified variants were tested; neither matches s15.

The unified design (17b/17c) is deferred — k-threshold tuning or a validation-first redesign
would be needed before it could be competitive. Out of scope for v2.6.2.

## Post-Ship Findings (17d/17e — extension beyond original v2.6.2 scope)

### Finding 4 — 17d (validated-antecedent coref gate): wrong hypothesis

17d added a gate: coref links are only accepted if the antecedent component is unvalidated (not yet
in the validated pool). Hypothesis: coref FPs come from unvalidated components. Result: 86.8% macro
(−0.3pp vs 17c, not a real improvement). Root cause disproven: coref FPs are FROM validated
components (gate never fires on the FPs). 17d is discardable.

### Finding 5 — 17e (validated coref): **BREAKTHROUGH — 92.3% GPT, +3.2pp vs s15**

17e adds a coref-specific single-pass validation gate: after coref resolves mentions, coref-sourced
candidate links pass through the same Phase 4 evidence-bundle validation as framing-sourced links
(but single-pass, not multi-pass). This cuts FPs from 43 → 14 and recovers +5.2pp over 17c.

**Key insight**: In 17c, coref links bypassed Phase 4 validation entirely. 17e closes this gap.
The architecture now enforces: ALL links (framing AND coref alike) pass the same quality gate.
This is the "unified validation" concept — cleaner ICSE story and empirically superior to s15.

TM improvement (+7.3pp): coref FPs in TM were validated components mis-resolved by coref; gate now
catches these. JAB recovery (90%→100%): coref over-fire on tiny doc fully suppressed by gate.

**FP breakdown (17e):** TM 8 (5 multi_framing + 3 coref), BBB 5 (3 multi_framing + 2 coref), TS 1 (coref), MS 0, JAB 0.
Remaining multi_framing FPs (TM 5 + BBB 3) are candidates for 17f (C-privileged union).

## Finding 6 — 17f (code-path filter): negative on both backends

17f adds Phase 4b: LLM code-path filter dropping dot-separated package-path-only mentions.
Hypothesis: addresses remaining multi_framing FPs (TM 5, BBB 3).
Result: 17f GPT 92.0% (−0.3pp vs 17e, FP 14→12), 17f Claude 92.0% (−1.4pp, FP 15→21).
The filter kills TPs on Claude: BBB 83.9%→79.0% (−4.9pp), TS −1.8pp. FP INCREASES on Claude.
**17f is a negative result. 17e remains the best variant on both backends.**
