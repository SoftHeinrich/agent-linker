## Findings (the milestone's research output)

- **Dropping training costs nothing.** s15 (no-training) GPT 89.1 == trained s14_voyager (89.11).
  Training delivered no lift over the axiom floor — validates the v2.6.1 thesis.
- **FP fixes work on Claude, are inert on GPT-5.4** (FP attribution): Claude removed Cause-A's 6
  targets, Cause-C's cluster (5+→1), 2/3 of Cause-B (Storage@125 residual); GPT kept all targets
  (reads nuanced axiom rules literally — documented pattern). TM FP: Claude 17→6, GPT 17.
- **BBB Claude 83.5 ≈ canonical s13_min (~85)**; +5.9pp over GPT. BBB weakness is mostly a GPT
  backend effect, not an s15 defect.
- **Pre-existing gaps carried forward (NOT regressions):** trim tax (early rich s2–s12 hit BBB
  0.90–0.96 on Claude vs s15 0.835); canonical gap (s15 Claude 92.7 vs s13_min 95.06, −2.3pp);
  BBB FN ceiling (14 FN) — these map to frozen v2.7 (Tier-C + partial-injection).
