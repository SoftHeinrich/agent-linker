## Thesis (reframed 2026-06-02)

**Drop training.** The Voyager training loop (learned per-slot bank) is abandoned in favour of the
axiom-only floor. The axioms — improved by three root-cause FP fixes — are used directly, with no
learned bank, no training tiers, no Assessor/oracle/cross-split machinery. The linker "falls back
to the old (pre-Voyager) pipeline + axiom prompts". This ships as a new clean standalone linker
**`s_linker15`**, and the now-dead Voyager training infrastructure is removed.

Justification: across v2.3→v2.6 the trained bank delivered only ~+1.6pp over the axiom-only floor
at high cost ($60–111/milestone) and WEAK verdicts, with split-fragility (BBB variance ±3.8pp
swamping ≤1pp signal). The axiom-only floor (88.99%, B-variant) is competitive without training
spend or split-fragility. v2.6.1 commits to the floor.

## Semver Rationale

Backward-compatible: a new linker variant + infra removal + axiom prompt refinement. No change to
the canonical artifact (`s_linker13_min`). No new external feature. Base = v2.6 axiom (shipped).
Therefore **PATCH → v2.6.1**. (Removing infra is large in LOC but is dead-code deletion, not a
breaking API change — still patch-level for this research project's line.)
