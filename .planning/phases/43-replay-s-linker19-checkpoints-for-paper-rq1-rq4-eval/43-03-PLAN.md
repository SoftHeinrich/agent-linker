<objective>
Populate the two RQ1 LaTeX tables (`writing/working/tables/metrics_sad-sam.tex` and `metrics_sad-code.tex`) from Plan 02's per-backend CSVs. Per D-03 the tables are wide (5 project rows + 1 Macro row, two column groups: Claude | GPT-5.4) and Claude-first.

Per D-02 the formatter lives in `evaluation/` (`transarc-emp/src/paper/`) and is stdlib-only — it imports nothing from `agent-linker`. It only reads CSV files Plan 02 already produced.

Output: one new formatter + two populated TeX files.
</objective>
