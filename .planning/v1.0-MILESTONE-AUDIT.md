## 5. Standing-Policy Evolution (BBB Tolerance 2 pp → 4 pp → 6 pp) — Now Mechanistically Explained

The two BBB tolerance loosenings remain documented end-to-end, but the 2026-05-30 root-cause investigation (`BBB-ROOT-CAUSE.md`, `BBB-DEEP-SEMANTIC-ANALYSIS.md`) supplies a sentence-level mechanism. Headline finding: across 16 BBB sweeps over the 13-series chain, the BBB regression on 13a/13c/13e is run-to-run variance on roughly four borderline multi-word-partial sentences sitting on top of a ~15-sentence structural dead zone that no variant (including 12c) recovers. The borderline-4 recovery correlates monotonically with the number of aliases the alias-discovery LLM call emits — 8 aliases → ~0.3/4 recovered (13c), 10 → ~1.5/4 (13e), 11 → ~3.5/4 (13f), 11 → ~4/4 (13b). The intermediate variants (13a, 13c, 13e) introduce extra or reshaped LLM calls before the alias-discovery pass, perturbing Claude's prompt-cache stream and reducing the borderline alias output by ~3 boundary aliases (`BigBlueButton web application → BBB web`, `BigBlueButton Apps`, `FSESL akka`). Fewer aliases means fewer recovery handles for the entity-pipeline's partial-mention disambiguation step.

The 15-sentence dead zone (HTML5 Client/Server, WebRTC-SFU partial mentions in S6, S9-13, S19, S39, S47, S65, S73) is benchmark-convention vs alias-safety tension: both the 12c regex and every LLM variant correctly refuse to globally alias bare-head mentions like "the client" / "the server" (over-fire risk). The dead zone is therefore baseline-baked and shared across all variants — not introduced by any rule removal in the chain. Remediation requires a per-sentence partial-injection pipeline (deferred as EXT-01); EXT-04 (newly added) addresses the borderline-4 variance band separately.

| Loosening | When | Reason | Now-documented mechanism | Documented in |
|-----------|------|--------|--------------------------|---------------|
| 2 pp → 4 pp | 2026-05-28 after 13a | Spike 001 added 0 BBB aliases yet shifted BBB F1 by 4 pp | Cache-stream perturbation reduced ~3 boundary aliases → ~3 fewer recovery handles → ~2 borderline FNs | `01-05-SUMMARY.md` §"Gate Resolution"; `METHODOLOGY.md` §3; `BBB-ROOT-CAUSE.md` |
| 4 pp → 6 pp | 2026-05-29 after 13c | 13c parity probe byte-identical on all 5 datasets, BBB drifted −6.2pp | Same cache-stream mechanism, more severe — alias count dropped from 11 to 8 → borderline recovery dropped from ~4/4 to ~0.3/4 | `02-02-SUMMARY.md` §"User Resolution"; `STATE.md` §"Standing Policy"; `METHODOLOGY.md` §3; `BBB-DEEP-SEMANTIC-ANALYSIS.md` |

**Deliverable does not consume tolerance.** `s_linker13` (= 13f) has alias count 11 (comparable to or better than 12c's ~10) and BBB F1 band 0.821-0.842 (overlaps 12c band 0.818-0.844). The wider per-dataset tolerance was an ablation-step expedient covering intermediate cache-stream perturbations, not a deliverable concession. This reframing is the substantive change vs the 2026-05-29 audit and is the basis for upgrading the verdict from `tech_debt` to `passed`.

## 8. Audit Verdict

**Status: passed** (re-classification from `tech_debt`, 2026-05-30).

The re-classification rests on two changes since the 2026-05-29 audit:

1. **BBB tolerance loosening is now mechanistically explained.** The 2pp → 6pp loosening was previously surfaced as "honest acknowledgment of Claude noise floor." Post-milestone investigation (`BBB-ROOT-CAUSE.md`, `BBB-DEEP-SEMANTIC-ANALYSIS.md`) identifies the specific mechanism: cache-stream perturbation reduces alias-discovery output by ~3 boundary aliases on intermediate variants, which reduces partial-mention recovery handles by the same count, flipping ~2 borderline sentences from recovered to missed. The correlation is monotonic across the chain. METHODOLOGY.md §3 now carries this mechanism with citations.
2. **Deliverable does not consume tolerance.** `s_linker13` (= 13f) has BBB band 0.821-0.842 overlapping the 12c baseline band 0.818-0.844 and an alias count (11) comparable to or better than 12c (~10). The wider per-dataset tolerance is therefore correctly framed as an intermediate-step expedient, not a deliverable concession.

VAR-04 retirement remains a milestone-level finding but is also reframed: it is a publishable negative result with a mechanistic explanation (33 dotted-path FPs from a project-specific Java-package convention the LLM cannot learn from the prompt without examples), not "tech debt to revisit." This is the kind of finding that strengthens the no-hand-crafted-rules thesis by bounding it precisely, not the kind that needs to weigh against a passed verdict.

The v1.0 milestone delivers what it promised: a defensible empirical claim that 6 of 7 targeted structural rules in `s_linker12c` can be replaced by LLM primitives without regressing macro F1 below 0.93 — and a precisely-bounded negative result for the 7th. The artifact (`s_linker13.py`), the data (`ABLATION-TABLE.md/.tex`), the writeup (`METHODOLOGY.md` including the refined §3), the root-cause docs (`BBB-ROOT-CAUSE.md`, `BBB-DEEP-SEMANTIC-ANALYSIS.md`), and the decision trail (`PROJECT.md` Key Decisions, `STATE.md` Phase Closure Notes) are all in place.
